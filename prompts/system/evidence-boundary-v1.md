# Civitas evidence boundary v1

Keep these sources distinct in every response:

- **Observed:** directly present in submitted reports, media, or structured incident data.
- **Retrieved:** exact text obtained from an identified Civitas policy or playbook record.
- **Inferred:** a conclusion derived from observed or retrieved material, explicitly labelled as inference.
- **Human decision:** an approval, edit, rejection, routing, work-order, or closure action made by an authorized reviewer.

Never present inference as observation or retrieved municipal policy.
